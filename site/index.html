Everything starts with a key.

Gemini Tray lives in the system tray. Each feature has a global shortcut, so you never have to look for a browser tab. All shortcuts can be changed.

Alt+V

Voice message

Press, speak, done. Gemini's own speech recognition writes your message into the prompt, and it is sent – or left for you to check first.

Alt+Shift+V

Dictation to your clipboard

Speak in any app. The recognised text lands in your clipboard, ready for Ctrl + V in an email, a chat or a document.

Alt+Shift+S

Screenshot & Ask

Drag a frame around an error message, a chart or a paragraph. The image is attached to your Gemini prompt – just type your question.

Alt+J

Saved prompts

Keep the instructions you use every day – “explain briefly”, “fix my grammar” – and put one in front of your next message, typed or spoken.

Alt+N

Your shortcuts, your way

New chat, show and hide, every feature – change any combination in the settings. If another app already uses a key, Gemini Tray tells you instead of failing silently.

The whole Gemini, not a cut-down copy.

The window shows gemini.google.com itself, so Gems, history, Canvas, Deep Research and image generation work exactly as in your browser.

  • No API key, no extra costYou sign in with your Google account and use your own plan.
  • Nothing gets lostIf inserting into Gemini fails, your text or image goes to the clipboard and you're told so.
  • No telemetryScreenshots are only sent when you confirm a selection. Your session stays on your PC.
  • Fits into Windows 11Light and dark mode follow Windows. Pin the window on top, set its opacity.
  • Starts with WindowsOptional autostart, minimized to the tray, ready when you press the key.
  • Updates itselfNew versions install from inside the app. Interface in English and German.

Set up in a minute.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

  1. Download the installer from the latest release and run it.

    Windows SmartScreen may ask once – choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

  2. Sign in to your Google account in the Gemini window.

    If Google refuses the sign-in there, use “Sign in with browser” in the tray menu.

  3. Press Alt + Space.

    That's it. The tray icon has everything else, including the settings.
